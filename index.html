A Decade of Research and Direct Human Support

Majd Hawily

For nearly a decade, Majd has worked at the intersection of research and direct human support, across psychology labs, community organizations, and settlement agencies in Lebanon and Canada. Her research has spanned cross-cultural memory and cognition, immigrant and newcomer mental health, and the lived experience of women navigating chronic illness and perfectionism across cultures, always returning to the same underlying question: how culture and context shape who a person becomes. That question is now leading her toward clinical training, working from a depth-oriented lens with people rebuilding a sense of self somewhere far from where they started.
