Psychological Researcher, MSc

Majd Hawily

Majd is a researcher specializing in cross-cultural psychology and mental health among newcomer, immigrant, and Indigenous communities. She designs mixed-methods studies — statistical modeling in R and SPSS alongside qualitative analysis in NVivo — that measure the real-world impact of educational and support programs, turning complex psychological questions into evidence that research teams and therapeutic organizations can put into practice.
